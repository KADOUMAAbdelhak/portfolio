SwiftPay — Digital Payments Platform

Production digital payments web platform built from scratch at Fingletek

Overview

SwiftPay is a production digital payments web platform whose public product experience presents a modern payments product for sending, receiving, and managing payments—positioned for individuals and businesses across Africa.

I independently built the platform from scratch as part of my work at Fingletek. The publicly inspectable surface is the live product website at swiftpayng.com: a responsive full-stack product presentation covering features, how-it-works flows, technology messaging, company context, and contact entry points. This case study focuses on the engineering ownership behind that production software—not marketing metrics or regulatory claims.

The Product

The live public site is a production Next.js application that communicates the SwiftPay payments product. Observable public surfaces include:

  • Responsive homepage and navigation across Features, How It Works, Technology, About, and Contact
  • Product messaging around secure payments, real-time transfers, ledger-oriented accounting, reconciliation, and scalable infrastructure
  • A clear three-step product journey: create an account, send or receive money, and track transactions
  • Illustrative payments-oriented UI presentation (balances and activity) used to communicate the product experience
  • Contact and enquiry entry points as the public call-to-action pathway

Public routes for login, registration, dashboard, or interactive send/receive screens were not exposed for inspection. This page does not treat illustrative hero balances or marketing statistics as real operational metrics.

The Product Need

The product needed a coherent digital payments platform experience: a public-facing web product that could present send, receive, and manage-payment workflows, and a maintainable application stack behind that experience.

The engineering challenge was end-to-end ownership—frontend implementation, backend/application functionality, APIs and integrations, persistence, UX, and production deployment—delivered as working software rather than disconnected marketing pages.

What I Built

I designed and delivered the production platform end to end:

  • Responsive public product frontend and payments-oriented UX
  • Backend/application functionality supporting the payments product
  • API-oriented workflows and service integration
  • Persistence and data handling for application state
  • Production deployment, debugging, and ongoing maintenance

Frontend & User Experience

I implemented the responsive public product experience visible on the live site, including:

  • Responsive layout and sectioned navigation across the product website
  • Clear product storytelling for features, how-it-works, and technology themes
  • Payments-oriented visual presentation (illustrative balance and activity UI)
  • Contact/enquiry forms and call-to-action pathways
  • Polished, scannable UX suited to a production fintech product surface

The public website is delivered with Next.js (React), verified from the live deployment asset paths and response headers.

Backend & Integrations

Alongside the public frontend, I built the application-side engineering required for a payments-oriented production product:

  • Backend application functionality for product workflows
  • API-oriented interfaces connecting clients and services
  • Persistence and data workflows supporting application state
  • Validation and application-state handling for payment-oriented flows
  • Integration between frontend, backend, and supporting services

The public Technology section describes a Go microservices backend with PostgreSQL persistence. Specific provider names, private endpoints, schemas, and routing internals are omitted here.
