Muflihun Concepts — Company Website & Product Hub

Production company website and product hub built from scratch at Fingletek

Overview

Muflihun Concepts is a software and IT solutions company whose public web platform presents digital services, a product hub, delivery process messaging, and contact pathways for organizations exploring web, mobile, research, and IT work.

I independently built the production website from scratch as part of my work at Fingletek. The live platform at muflihunconcepts.com is a multi-page responsive public presence—Home, About, Services, Products, and Contact—designed to communicate the company offering and route visitors toward next actions. This case study focuses on that frontend product ownership and production delivery, not unverified business metrics.

The Product

The public site is a company website and product hub. Observable surfaces include:

  • Home — company positioning, service overview, product highlights, and how-we-work messaging
  • About — company overview, mission/vision, and partnership-oriented positioning
  • Services — Web Development, Mobile Development, Research & Innovation, and IT Solutions
  • Products — public hub entries for 9jaShares and SwiftPay with outbound explore links
  • Contact — contact information, enquiry form pathway, FAQ, and location context

The platform serves as the company’s public web presence and product catalogue entry point. It does not expose private admin tooling or authenticated product dashboards on this domain.

The Product Need

The company needed a coherent public web presence that could explain who Muflihun Concepts is, what services it offers, which digital products it powers, and how prospective clients start a conversation.

The engineering task was to design and ship a maintainable multi-page website with clear information architecture, responsive UX, reusable presentation patterns, and production deployment—without treating the site as a disposable brochure.

What I Built

I designed and delivered the production public platform end to end:

  • Responsive multi-page company website (Home, About, Services, Contact)
  • Product-hub presentation for powered digital products
  • Navigation, content architecture, and call-to-action pathways
  • Contact/enquiry user flow and supporting page UX
  • Production deployment, debugging, and iterative maintenance

Frontend & User Experience

The public implementation is frontend- and content-led. I implemented the responsive product UX, including:

  • Responsive multi-page layout and shared navigation
  • Clear visual hierarchy for company messaging and sectioned content
  • Reusable service and product presentation patterns
  • How-we-work delivery messaging (Discover → Design → Build → Support)
  • Contact/CTA pathways from homepage through dedicated contact page
  • Motion and interaction polish suited to a production company site

Verified frontend stack on the live deployment: HTML/CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, AOS, and custom site styles/scripts.

Contact Pathways & Product Links

Public inspection did not show a separate authenticated backend application, CMS-driven API surface, or database-backed admin area on this domain. What is present and verified:

  • Dedicated contact page with company contact details and an enquiry form pathway
  • Mailto-based message submission using the visitor’s email client
  • Outbound product-hub links to 9jaShares and SwiftPay public product surfaces

No private APIs, persistence schemas, CRM integrations, or authentication flows are claimed for this website.
